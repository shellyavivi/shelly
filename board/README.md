# Moodwall

An endless, Pinterest-style inspiration wall for logo and brand identity work.

**Open it:** after GitHub Pages is turned on (Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → `main`, `/ (root)`),
it lives at `https://shellyavivi.github.io/shelly/board/`. Locally, open `board/index.html` in a browser.

- **Wall:** categories (Teddy Lab, logos, identity, toys, packaging, type, craft, mascots, photography,
  architecture, art). Each one streams live, endlessly, from Are.na, Wikimedia Commons, the Art Institute of
  Chicago and the Cleveland Museum of Art. Every card links back to its source.
- **Pins:** paste any Behance, Instagram, Pinterest, YouTube or Vimeo link and it becomes a live embed.
  Any other link becomes a card with a page preview. Pins stay in your browser. Use **Export pins** and paste
  them into `pinned` in `data.js` to keep them for good.
- **Dig deeper:** opens the same search on Behance, Pinterest, Dribbble and the matching Instagram hashtags.
- **Studios:** 36 international studios with live previews of their sites, filterable by country.
- **Saved:** tap ♥ on anything to keep it.

Edit `data.js` to change categories, search terms, studios or pinned references.
